CDS AutoFill is a browser extension designed to assist pharmacists with prescription data entry. It extracts prescription text, presents a review step, and fills supported CDS fields only after pharmacist confirmation.

Built for practical pharmacy workflow

The extension assists with repetitive data-entry tasks while keeping the pharmacist in control of every prescription.

📋

Prescription parsing

Extracts supported patient, prescription, physician, institution and medication fields from pasted prescription text.

🧠

Smart duration

Compares written duration with a local calculation based on quantity, strength, dose and frequency. Conflicts are sent for manual review.

✅

Review before fill

Parsed values are shown to the pharmacist before supported CDS fields are populated.

🔒

Local processing

Prescription and patient data are processed locally by the extension. They are not sent to an external prescription-processing server.

📊

Local logs & export

Supported usage logs can remain in the browser and be exported locally by the user.

🧑‍⚕️

Pharmacist-controlled

CDS AutoFill is a data-entry assistant. It does not replace professional judgment or make dispensing decisions.

Privacy by design

Patient names, identifiers, diagnoses and prescription details are intended to stay on the pharmacist's device during normal use. A commercial licensing system may transmit only licensing-related information such as a license key, installation identifier, extension version and license status. It must not receive patient or prescription data.
