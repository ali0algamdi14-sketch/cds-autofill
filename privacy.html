Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

1. What CDS AutoFill does

CDS AutoFill is a browser extension that assists pharmacists with prescription data entry. It parses prescription text locally, displays a review step, and can populate supported fields after pharmacist confirmation.

2. Patient and prescription data

Patient names, patient identifiers, diagnoses, prescription identifiers, physician information, medication details and other prescription content are processed locally on the user's device during normal operation. CDS AutoFill is not designed to transmit this information to an external prescription-processing server.

3. Local storage

The extension may store operational logs and settings locally in the browser. Users may export supported logs locally. Local browser data remains subject to the browser profile and device security controls.

4. Licensing

A commercial version may use an external licensing provider. Licensing requests may include a license key, a randomly generated installation identifier, extension version and license status. Patient and prescription data must not be included in licensing requests.

5. Professional responsibility

CDS AutoFill is a data-entry assistant and does not make dispensing decisions. Pharmacists are responsible for reviewing prescription information, confirming accuracy and complying with applicable laws, professional standards and institutional procedures.

6. Third-party services

If licensing or payment services are enabled, those providers may process licensing or transaction information under their own privacy terms. CDS AutoFill should minimize data shared with such services to what is required for licensing or payment operations.

7. Government affiliation

CDS AutoFill is independent software and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of SFDA, Raqeeb, or any government authority.

8. Contact

For privacy or support inquiries, contact information will be provided on the official distribution page before public commercial release.
