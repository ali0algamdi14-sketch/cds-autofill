What it does

Less repetitive entry. More focused review.

CDS AutoFill is designed to reduce repetitive prescription data entry while keeping review and submission with the pharmacist.

Smart prescription extraction

Reads the copied prescription text, identifies supported fields, and presents them for review before filling.

Patient Name→Preserved in source order

Local-first processing

Prescription data is processed on the pharmacist's device. The patient-data workflow does not require external processing.

Smart Duration

Uses current-prescription instructions as a local heuristic to help calculate duration for review.

Prescription session log

Start a local counting session, track processed prescriptions, clear the log, and export supported records to CSV.

Review before fill

Extracted values stay editable. The pharmacist reviews the prescription before supported CDS fields are filled.

Built around CDS workflow

Focused on practical prescription-entry steps without turning the extension into an automated dispensing decision maker.

Simple workflow

FAQ

Clear by design.

A few important points about what CDS AutoFill is — and what it is not.

Does CDS AutoFill submit prescriptions automatically?

No. It assists with review and supported field entry. The pharmacist reviews the data and remains responsible for the workflow.

Does it send patient data to an external AI service?

The patient prescription parsing workflow is designed to process data locally on the device.

Is CDS AutoFill an official SFDA, Raqeeb, or Ministry of Health product?

No. CDS AutoFill is independent software and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SFDA, Raqeeb, the Ministry of Health, or any government entity.
